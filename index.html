Executive View

F.R.I.E.N.D. is an AI-assisted operational intelligence and workforce development platform designed to support associate onboarding, leadership effectiveness, operational execution, and scalable division-wide performance across the King Soopers Division.

Associate Development

Guided onboarding, role-based learning, and daily support.

Leadership Effectiveness

Better visibility, coaching, prioritization, and accountability.

Operational Excellence

Clear execution standards across store, district, and division levels.

Division Intelligence

Performance dashboards, trend visibility, and scalable decision support.

Recommendation: approve a structured pre-pilot to validate feasibility before any broad rollout investment.

1. Pre-Pilot

The Pre-Pilot phase reduces risk by aligning stakeholders, validating technology readiness, defining success criteria, and collecting baseline metrics before live-store testing begins.

Current State

Assess leadership routines, training gaps, execution variance, communication channels, and technology usage.

Stakeholder Alignment

Align division leadership, district leadership, store leaders, department leaders, and support partners.

Technology Readiness

Validate TC-52 workflow, device access, connectivity, dashboard needs, permissions, and data requirements.

Exit Criteria

Pilot stores selected, metrics approved, support plan completed, and launch readiness confirmed.

Pre-Pilot AreaRequired Action
LeadershipDefine ownership, review cadence, and approval gates.
OperationsMap daily task execution and department workflows.
TrainingConnect Fresh Start, department learning, and leadership routines.
DataIdentify baseline metrics for shrink, labor, training, productivity, and adoption.

Pre-pilot costs should be treated as a controlled discovery investment to determine whether F.R.I.E.N.D. is operationally, financially, and technologically scalable.

$15K-$40K

$20K-$50K

Support Labor

Estimated Pre-Pilot Range: $70K-$175K depending on internal resources, vendor involvement, and technical integration depth.

3. Pilot Program

The pilot should run for 3-6 months across one low-volume, one medium-volume, and one high-volume store to validate adoption, performance impact, and scalability.

Low Volume Store

Test usability, onboarding, and simple workflow guidance.

Medium Volume Store

Validate consistency across departments and store leadership routines.

High Volume Store

8. Red Team Analysis

The Red Team section identifies implementation risks before rollout and defines mitigation strategies for each major concern.

RiskMitigation
Low AdoptionBuild with store leader and associate feedback before scaling.
Technology FrictionTest TC-52 usability and support workflows during pilot.
Data Quality IssuesValidate source systems and define data ownership.

9. 10-Point Rollout

  1. Secure division leadership alignment.
  2. Define pilot governance and success metrics.
  3. Select low, medium, and high-volume pilot stores.
  4. Finalize technology and TC-52 readiness.
  5. Train store leaders and assistant store leaders.
  6. Train department leaders and associates.
  7. Collect daily and weekly pilot performance data.
  8. Review pilot results with district leadership.
  9. Expand through a 20-30 store regional rollout.
  10. Scale across the King Soopers Division.
