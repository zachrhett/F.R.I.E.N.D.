1. Pre-Pilot

The pre-pilot phase establishes scope, stakeholders, technology needs, success metrics, and operational readiness before any live-store deployment.

WorkstreamPurpose
Current-State ReviewAssess existing store leadership routines, training gaps, associate workflows, and execution variance.
Stakeholder AlignmentEngage division leadership, district leadership, store leaders, department leaders, technology partners, and training teams.
Success CriteriaDefine measurable targets for adoption, training completion, shrink reduction, task execution, and associate support.
Technology ReadinessEvaluate TC-52 compatibility, data feeds, dashboard access, privacy requirements, and support model.

2. Pre-Pilot Cost

This section estimates the investment required to prepare F.R.I.E.N.D. for a controlled pilot.

Platform Development

Prototype refinement, workflow mapping, dashboard structure, AI guidance logic.

Hardware Review

TC-52 readiness, device access, battery/load testing, connectivity validation.

Training Materials

Store leader, department leader, and associate-facing rollout materials.

Support Labor

Project coordination, pilot store support, troubleshooting, leadership check-ins.

Pre-pilot costs should be treated as controlled discovery investment before broader regional or division rollout commitments.

3. Pilot Program

The pilot should test F.R.I.E.N.D. across three store environments: low volume, medium volume, and high volume.

Store TypePurposeLeadership Language
Low VolumeTest usability, training support, and baseline task execution.Store Leader / Assistant Store Leader
Medium VolumeValidate workflow consistency and department execution support.Store Leadership Team
High VolumeStress-test scalability, exception handling, and real-time guidance.Store Leadership Team + District Support

6. ROI

8. Red Team Analysis

This section identifies possible risks, objections, and failure points before implementation.

RiskMitigation
Low AdoptionBuild with store leader and associate feedback before scaling.
Technology FrictionTest TC-52 usability and support workflows during pilot.
Data Quality IssuesValidate source systems and define clear data ownership.
Change FatiguePosition F.R.I.E.N.D. as support for existing work, not another task burden.

9. 10-Point Rollout

  1. Secure division leadership alignment.
  2. Define pilot governance and success metrics.
  3. Select low, medium, and high-volume pilot stores.
  4. Finalize technology and TC-52 readiness.
  5. Train store leaders and assistant store leaders.
  6. Train department leaders and associates.
  7. Collect daily and weekly pilot performance data.
  8. Review pilot results with district leadership.
  9. Expand through a 20-30 store regional rollout.
  10. Scale across the King Soopers Division.
