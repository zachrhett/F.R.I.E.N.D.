1. Pre-Pilot

Purpose

Define scope, select pilot stores, identify workflows, establish success metrics, and prepare leadership alignment before technical build.

Primary Actions

  • Select low, medium, and high-volume stores
  • Map department workflows
  • Identify data sources
  • Define leadership reporting needs
  • Create pilot success standards

2. Pre-Pilot Cost

3. Pilot Program

Low Volume Store

Tests simplicity, ease of use, and lean staffing support.

Medium Volume Store

Tests daily operational consistency and balanced workload.

High Volume Store

Tests stress conditions, speed, leadership visibility, and scalability.

4. Regional Rollout

Expand to 20–30 stores using lessons learned from the pilot phase, structured leader training, and measurable performance tracking.

5. Division Rollout

Scale across all 155 King Soopers Division stores with standardized training, department workflows, leadership dashboards, and continuous improvement cycles.

6. ROI

ROI is driven by training efficiency, labor productivity, cost avoidance, improved execution, reduced rework, and better leadership visibility.

7. Inaction

The cost of inaction includes hidden labor loss, training drift, execution inconsistency, avoidable waste, and continued leadership overload.

8. Red Team

Key risks include adoption resistance, data quality concerns, implementation cost, training burden, and overreliance on technology.
