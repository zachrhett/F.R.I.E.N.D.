Pre-Pilot Purpose

Define the scope, choose the correct stores, identify stakeholders, establish success metrics, and prepare the operating environment before technical build or pilot launch.

Required Work

  • Select low, medium, and high-volume stores.
  • Map department workflows.
  • Identify data sources.
  • Document current training gaps.
  • Define leadership reporting needs.

3-Store Pilot Program

The pilot should run 3–6 months across one low-volume, one medium-volume, and one high-volume store. The goal is to validate usability, operational impact, leadership value, and training effectiveness before scaling.


Low Volume

Tests simplicity, ease of use, and lean staffing support.

Medium Volume

Tests daily operational consistency and balanced workload.

High Volume

Tests stress conditions, speed, leadership visibility, and scalability.

Regional Rollout

After pilot validation, expand to 20–30 stores with structured support, lessons learned, and leader training. This phase proves scalability before division-wide implementation.


PhaseFocusResult
Wave 1Early adopter storesTraining model refined
Wave 2Operationally diverse storesScalability tested
Wave 3Regional stabilizationLeadership confidence

Division Rollout

Cost of Inaction

The largest risk is not only direct cost. It is the hidden compounding loss from inconsistent execution, preventable training gaps, turnover drag, leadership overload, and missed operational insight.


Hidden Labor Loss

Time spent searching, correcting, repeating, and explaining.

Training Drift

Different stores teach different standards over time.

Leadership Strain

Leaders lose time managing avoidable confusion.
