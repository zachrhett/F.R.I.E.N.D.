Executive Summary

F.R.I.E.N.D. is not simply a dashboard. It is a retail intelligence and development system that turns operational data into daily guided action.

Strategic Purpose
  • Improve execution consistency across stores and departments.
  • Translate data into clear associate, department, and store priorities.
  • Support leadership development through guided coaching and training prompts.
  • Create visibility for store leaders, district leaders, and division leadership.
Positioning Statement

F.R.I.E.N.D. connects performance, people development, operational consistency, and scalable decision support into one operating framework.

Current State Assessment

Retail operations often suffer from fragmented data, competing priorities, inconsistent training, and delayed decisions.

Data Fragmentation

Leaders spend time gathering information instead of acting on it.

Training Separation

Training is often disconnected from the moment of work.

Execution Variation

Stores and departments may perform differently without a shared operating rhythm.

Opportunity

A common system can reduce friction and improve clarity.

Strategic Opportunity

The opportunity is to create a division-level system that connects execution, leadership, training, and performance into one repeatable model.

Pilot Plan

The recommended pilot should validate behavior change, measurable execution improvement, and leadership usability before broader rollout.

Phase 1: Pilot Design
  • Select three stores: low, medium, and high volume.
  • Define baseline metrics before launch.
  • Train store leaders and department leaders.
Phase 2: Execution
  • Deploy daily mission prompts.
  • Use dashboard reviews during leader walks.
  • Capture adoption and feedback weekly.
Phase 3: Measurement
  • Compare shrink, sales, markdown effectiveness, training completion, and task completion.
  • Document workflow friction and associate experience.
  • Prepare rollout recommendation.
