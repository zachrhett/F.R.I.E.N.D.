Executive Summary

  • Unifies operational visibility across stores, departments, and leadership levels.
  • Supports daily execution through task clarity, labor guidance, and performance dashboards.
  • Creates a scalable pilot-to-division rollout pathway.

Pre-Pilot

  • Define pilot scope, success metrics, and leadership ownership.
  • Select low, medium, and high-volume stores.
  • Map current process gaps before integration.

Pre-Pilot Cost

  • Technology review and device readiness.
  • Training preparation and implementation labor.
  • Data setup, reporting templates, and support structure.

Pilot Program

  • Run a 3–6 month pilot across three store types.
  • Collect execution, labor, sales, shrink, and adoption data.
  • Refine workflows before scaling.

Regional Rollout

  • Expand to 20–30 stores after pilot validation.
  • Standardize leadership routines and reporting cadence.
  • Adapt execution model by store volume and department needs.

Division Rollout

  • Scale across the King Soopers division.
  • Build training, support, and accountability systems.
  • Use pilot and regional data to reduce rollout risk.

ROI Analysis

  • Measure labor efficiency, shrink reduction, execution consistency, and training improvement.
  • Compare pilot costs against measurable operational gains.
  • Track ROI from pilot through division adaptation.

Cost of Inaction

  • Continued inefficiency from disconnected systems.
  • Lost execution consistency across departments.
  • Hidden costs from training gaps, shrink, and missed opportunities.

Red Team Analysis

  • Identify adoption resistance and operational friction.
  • Evaluate technology failure points and training risks.
  • Protect against overcomplexity before full rollout.

10-Point Rollout Plan

  • Define ownership.
  • Select pilot stores.
  • Prepare devices and dashboards.
  • Train leaders.
  • Launch pilot.
  • Collect data.
  • Refine process.
  • Expand regionally.
  • Prepare division rollout.
  • Measure and improve continuously.
