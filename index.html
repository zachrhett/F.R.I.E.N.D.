F.R.I.E.N.D. Proposal

King Soopers Division

F.R.I.E.N.D. is an AI-assisted operational support system designed to help store teams improve execution, training, communication, shrink reduction, labor productivity, and leadership visibility.

Executive Dashboard

  • Stores Supported: 155
  • Pilot Stores: 3
  • Pilot Length: 3–6 months
  • Training Goal: 95%+
  • Focus: Labor, Shrink, Training, Execution, ROI

1. Pre-Pilot

The pre-pilot phase prepares the organization for testing F.R.I.E.N.D. in a controlled environment before investing in a broader rollout.

2. Pre-Pilot Cost

Costs may include planning, leadership alignment, device preparation, training materials, workflow mapping, and technical setup.

3. Pilot Program

The pilot should be tested across three stores: one low-volume store, one medium-volume store, and one high-volume store.

4. Regional Rollout

After pilot results are reviewed, F.R.I.E.N.D. can be expanded to 20–30 stores with regional leadership support.

5. Division Rollout

A division rollout would scale the program across all 155 King Soopers stores with structured training, support, and performance tracking.

6. ROI

ROI should be measured through shrink reduction, labor productivity, training completion, execution consistency, and manager time savings.

7. Cost of Inaction

Inaction may result in continued shrink, inconsistent training, missed sales opportunities, poor communication, and unnecessary labor waste.

8. Red Team

Risks include poor adoption, training fatigue, inaccurate data, leadership resistance, added complexity, and technology costs.

9. 10 Point Rollout

  1. Leadership approval
  2. Define pilot stores
  3. Map department workflows
  4. Prepare devices
  5. Train leaders
  6. Train associates
  7. Launch pilot
  8. Track results
  9. Review ROI
  10. Scale across the division
