1

PRE-PILOT

Define scope, ownership, readiness, pilot stores, success metrics, and risk points before launch.

Framework

Select low, medium, and high-volume pilot stores. Map current execution gaps, communication gaps, training gaps, and leadership reporting needs.

Readiness

Confirm device access, reporting structure, department leader involvement, store leadership alignment, and weekly review cadence.

Output

A clean pilot charter with success metrics, owner list, timeline, and measurable expectations.
2

PRE-PILOT COST

Estimate the investment required before testing.

Technology

Device readiness, TC-52/Zebra access, dashboards, data templates, and technical support.

Labor

Training preparation, leadership review time, pilot support, reporting setup, and implementation meetings.

Controls

Cost should be staged and measured against operational improvement during the pilot.

REGIONAL ROLLOUT

Scale to 20–30 stores after pilot validation.

Regional Purpose

Validate support model, training cadence, store adaptation, leadership routines, and technology reliability across a larger store group.
5

DIVISION ROLLOUT

Prepare full King Soopers Division deployment.

Division Goal

Use pilot and regional learning to create a scalable, repeatable operating system across all 155 stores.
6

ROI ANALYSIS

Measure value through operational improvement.

ROI Drivers

Labor clarity, shrink reduction, training speed, task completion, compliance, and leadership visibility.

Measurement

Compare pilot costs against measurable gains and hidden loss reduction.

Long-Term Value

Create a repeatable improvement engine across the division.

COST OF INACTION
